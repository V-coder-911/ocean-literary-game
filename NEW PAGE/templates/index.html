Why Ocean Literacy Matters

Understanding ocean conditions is not just a scientific endeavor; it is essential for the lives and livelihoods of millions. By learning how waves, winds, temperatures, and alerts interact, we make choices that preserve our marine ecosystems and protect human lives.

Fishermen Livelihoods

Identifying Potential Fishing Zones (PFZ) increases yield while reducing fuel waste and avoiding sudden squalls.

Maritime Navigation

Ship captains navigate safely through high-wave warnings and cyclones, avoiding disastrous accidents at sea.

Disaster Preparedness

Being aware of tsunami indicators and storm surge alerts saves coastal communities during extreme events.
